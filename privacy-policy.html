Align privacy policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Align is designed to be private

Align has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app stores, the few ways information can leave your phone, and the control you have.

What Align stores on your phone

Your goals and daily time, reading progress, saved articles and highlights, mood check-ins and notes, balance check results, guided program progress and reflections, calm check ratings, your reminder time, read-aloud voice and speed, sound settings and display settings. Align has no server that receives this information, and the developer cannot see it.

Personal information you write

Mood notes, program reflections, balance results and calm ratings can be personal. They are only ever stored on your phone. Align never sends them anywhere.

Internet use

If the app is set up to receive new articles, it downloads a content file from the web. That request contains no personal information. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the hosting service, which Align does not see or store.

Reading aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech service. Most voices work entirely on the phone. If you choose an online voice in your phone's settings, the speech service (for example, Google) may process the article text under its own privacy policy. Only article text is read aloud, never your notes or reflections.

Backups

Android may back up app data to your own Google account, privately and protected by your Google account. Align and its developer cannot see these backups. You can turn this off in your phone's settings, usually under Google, then Backup. You can also export a backup file yourself from the You screen.

Reminders

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server sends it.

Crash reports

If Align crashes, technical details (app version, Android version, phone model and the error) are saved on your phone. The next time you open the app, you are asked whether to send them. They are sent only if you choose to, from your own email app, where you can read them first. They are used only to fix problems.

Your control

On the You screen, under Your data, you can export a backup of everything to a file you choose, restore it on another phone, reset your reading progress, or delete all your data at once. Uninstalling Align also deletes everything it stored on your phone.

What Align does not do

Align does not collect personal information, does not use advertising or analytics services, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Not professional advice

Align shares general wellbeing information. It is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological or financial advice. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a helpline; the app lists some on the You screen.

Children

Align is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at the address on Align's Google Play listing, with a new effective date.
